# Building Weapons and Tools

Weapons and hand tools are slender grouped objects built along one axis: a handle or grip, a guard or collar, and a blade, head, or barrel. Keep proportions believable and present the weapon so it reads clearly.

## Common weapons

Sword: a thin, long, stretched box for the blade, a short crossguard (a small box across the blade base), a cylindrical grip, and a pommel (a small sphere). Axe or hammer: a cylinder handle with a box or wedge head near the top. Spear: a long thin cylinder shaft with a small cone or pyramid tip. Gun: a box body, a cylinder barrel, a grip box, and a small sight. Shield: a flattened box or cylinder disc with a central boss (a sphere).

## Proportions and grouping

Group the whole weapon and name the parts (Blade, Crossguard, Grip, Pommel). A sword is about 0.9-1.1m long, with the blade much longer than the grip, and the blade kept thin. For glowing or magical weapons, give the blade an emissive colour and add a matching point light.

## Materials

Blades and metal: low roughness, high metalness, steel grey — or emissive for magic. Wood handles: matte brown. Leather grips: dark, high roughness. Gold pommels and guards: warm and metallic.

## Common failure modes

A single box standing in for a sword — separate blade, guard, grip, and pommel. A blade as thick as the grip — keep it thin. A floating weapon — rest it on a surface or stand it deliberately.
